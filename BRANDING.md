# Branding

`icon.png` is the Threads mark (the "@"-shaped Threads logo on a black square) as the
Threads website declares it for its pages, used only to identify this integration in
Flow Steward.

## Source

- Product: Threads, the social network of Meta Platforms, Inc. — <https://www.threads.com/>.
- Official asset: the 256 × 256 `apple-touch-icon` that <https://www.threads.com/> declares
  (`<link rel="apple-touch-icon" sizes="256x256" href="https://static.cdninstagram.com/rsrc.php/yn/r/fFvbm7ecCaM.webp">`),
  a WebP image, downloaded 2026-11-23.
- SHA-256 of the source: `8d355c41f322ecc4318cf8734c3c04a2183fb05ce74c70f88bf2483794ea3f0c`.

## What was done

- The WebP image was decoded and scaled from 256 × 256 to 128 × 128 pixels (Lanczos) and
  written as PNG, because Flow Steward takes a PNG icon. Nothing else was changed: no
  recolouring, cropping or editing.
- SHA-256 of `icon.png`: `fc8f2144788e1190187b0b70b083a281332bf906425bc23f83ab8f3b67e85f9b`.

## Use

Threads and its logo are trademarks of Meta Platforms, Inc. The icon is used only to show
which service this integration connects to; it does not imply endorsement by Meta.

## Meta's rules for the Threads logo

Source: Meta Brand Resource Center, "Threads Brand"
(<https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/threads/>), read 2026-11-24. The page says that
more detailed guidelines are coming soon; what it states now:

- Anyone using assets for Threads should use only the logos found on the Brand Resource
  Center site and follow its guidelines.
- The icon must be surrounded by a minimum clear space: the width of the icon divided by 4.
- Permission is needed for broadcast, radio, out-of-home advertising, or print larger than
  8.5 × 11 inches (A4). An icon inside a software product is none of these.
- The logo pack (a ZIP) is downloaded only after accepting Meta's guidelines and terms
  for use.

Conclusion: the rules do not ask for permission for this use, but they allow only the
logos of the Brand Resource Center. `icon.png` comes from the threads.com page icon, not
from the Brand Resource Center, so it does not meet that rule. It is left unchanged: the
logo pack needs Meta's terms accepted, which is the owner's decision, not this file's.
