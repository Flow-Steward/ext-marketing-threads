# Branding

`icon.png` is the Threads mark (the "@"-shaped Threads logo on a black square) as the
Threads website declares it for its pages, used only to identify this integration in
Flow Steward.

## Source

- Product: Threads, the social network of Meta Platforms, Inc. — <https://www.threads.com/>.
- Official asset: the 256 × 256 `apple-touch-icon` that <https://www.threads.com/> declares
  (`<link rel="apple-touch-icon" sizes="256x256" href="https://static.cdninstagram.com/rsrc.php/yn/r/fFvbm7ecCaM.webp">`),
  a WebP image, downloaded 2026-11-23.
- SHA-256 of the source: `8d355c41f322ecc4318cf8734c3c04a2183fb05ce74c70f88bf2483794ea3f0c`.

## What was done

- The WebP image was decoded and scaled from 256 × 256 to 128 × 128 pixels (Lanczos) and
  written as PNG, because Flow Steward takes a PNG icon. Nothing else was changed: no
  recolouring, cropping or editing.
- SHA-256 of `icon.png`: `fc8f2144788e1190187b0b70b083a281332bf906425bc23f83ab8f3b67e85f9b`.
